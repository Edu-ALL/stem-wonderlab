WHAT IS A MAKERSPACE?

A Movement of Student-led Innovation

COLLABORATING WITH INDONESIA MENGAJAR

Inspiring the Future Through Teaching

Indonesia Mengajar is a public initiative that invites society to actively participate in the movement to advance education in Indonesia. Indonesia Mengajar believes that the key to educational progress lies in the ecosystem in which a child grows — a community that cares about education. We believe that education is a shared responsibility. Collective effort from all layers of society will support the advancement of education in Indonesia. With the involvement of various parties, Indonesia Mengajar never works alone. That’s why Indonesia Mengajar serves as a bridge between elements within the educational ecosystem, actively encouraging public participation in education through various means — one of which is through collaboration with EduALL.

Learn more: www.indonesiamengajar.org

In collaboration with STEM+ Wonderlab, several student projects will be selected and donated to aid the learning experience in these underserved communities. This initiative provides a truly tangible way for students to see their efforts translate into a direct and meaningful national impact.

MATHnipulative

Clive is passionate about making mathematics engaging and accessible for young learners. Through his project, MATHnipulative, he develops creative and affordable DIY math manipulatives designed specifically for primary school students. By transforming abstract mathematical concepts into hands-on and concrete learning experiences, Clive aims to foster curiosity and build a strong foundational understanding of math. His goal is to empower teachers and parents with practical tools that make math both fun and meaningful, inspiring children to see the subject as an exciting part of their everyday lives. In the future, Clive envisions scaling MATHnipulative to reach more classrooms, supporting a new generation of confident young mathematicians.

Clive is passionate about strategic thinking and decision-making. Through his self-written book on game theory, he introduces complex concepts like the prisoner's dilemma and Nash equilibrium in a way that is accessible to high school students. His goal is to spark curiosity and show how game theory applies to everyday situations—from negotiations to social interactions. By making abstract theories approachable, Clive hopes to encourage more young people to explore the power of mathematics and critical thinking in understanding the world around them.

Clive
Temuhara

Temuhara is a skincare and bodycare brand inspired by Indonesia’s powerful herbs and healing traditions. Founded by Unaisa, a student passionate about organic materials and herbal science, Temuhara began as a personal exploration and has developed into a brand focused on creating gentle, effective products like lip balm and body butter, made with locally sourced natural ingredients. Guided by curiosity and care, Unaisa formulates each product to highlight the calming and therapeutic properties of native Indonesian plants. Temuhara reflects a mission to reconnect people with natural wellness through simple, nourishing self-care. It stands for quality, authenticity, and a return to nature’s quiet strength in everyday life.

Unaisa
JoyDrive

Andrea was inspired to a create meaningful learning experiences through play. By building a simple remote-controlled toy car allows children to gain hands-on opportunities to explore cause and effect, develop fine motor skills, and grasp basic mechanics in an engaging way. The joystick controller further enhances this by helping develop hand-eye coordination and precise control, making learning both fun and effective. This approach sparks curiosity and creativity, allowing kids to discover and learn through active interaction and experimentation—experiences that traditional methods may not provide.

Andrea
Aqua Vision

At Aquavision, Evan is developing a Machine Learning program that detects various kinds of plastic waste on the surface of different water bodies and conditions, with the endgoal of enhancing environmental monitoring efforts by accurately detecting plastic debris in oceans, rivers, and lakes, providing valuable data for cleanup initiatives and pollution research. With a passion for sustainability and technology, Evan is refining AquaVision to improve detection accuracy under diverse conditions, contributing to the global fight against marine plastic pollution while inspiring eco-conscious AI solutions, releasing late 2025-early 2026

Evan
Forces for Kids

Forces for Kids is an educational mobile app designed to make science fun and engaging for children. Inspired by her struggles growing up in understanding scientific concepts in primary school, Kiara created an app that simplifies learning science through a more interactive storytelling approach for kids.

In the app, children will join scientist Hannah on exciting missions to retrieve her stolen secret formula, where key forces are needed to help Hannah overcome obstacles. Along the way, children will explore and apply key physics concepts like gravity, friction, magnetism, and buoyancy (to name a few) to solve puzzles and overcome obstacles.

Forces for Kids transforms abstract science topics into memorable adventures that spark curiosity and deepen scientific understanding. Be it in the classroom or at home, Forces for Kids will empower children to learn by doing because it's always more fun when it comes to life.

Kiara
Affordable Prosthetic Arm for Under-Privileged Amputees

Ethan is passionate about innovation and practical engineering. Through his current project—a functional mechanical arm—he explores ways to make assistive technology more accessible and affordable. His goal is to design a low-cost, customizable prototype that can be adapted for educational purposes or daily use by individuals with limb differences. By combining creativity with technical skill, Ethan hopes to inspire other students to use engineering as a tool for social impact and inclusive design

Ethan
Remedi

Remedi is a wellness brand rooted in Indonesia’s longstanding tradition of natural healing. Founded by Elaine, a student with a strong interest in botanical science and gentle formulations, its balm was developed in response to the widespread use of petroleum jelly and camphor in commercial balms that are volatile and lipophilic, resulting in irritation. Recognizing the limited accessibility of natural alternatives due to their high cost and reliance on imported materials, Elaine formulated Remedi using locally sourced Indonesian ingredients such as cajuput oil for its gentle properties used in traditional Indonesian medicine. The result is an effective, affordable balm designed to support everyday relief, while promoting the use of safe, natural, and regionally available solutions.

Elaine Faythe Hartono
Dermapetix

Giselle deeply cares about animal health and believes in the power of natural medicine. Through this project, she developed a gentle, roll-on treatment designed to combat ringworm infections in pets using plant-based ingredients. Her formulation focuses on natural compounds known to break down the fungal cell wall, offering an alternative to chemical-based treatments that can irritate sensitive skin. With this product, she hopes to provide a safer, eco-conscious option for pet owners—while raising awareness about responsible pet care and the science behind fungal infections. Her project blends compassion, biology, and innovation to create something both practical and purposeful.

Giselle
Tastopia

Tastopia is a unique, and edible sensory learning play experience created by Jessica. Driven by curiosity and a passion for early child development, Jessica's sensory learning play aims to help toddlers and young children to further increase their motor skills, sensory processing skills, creativity/imagination, cognitive growth, and emotional regulation through transforming simple, safe food into unique and engaging hands on activities that allows children to touch, taste, and explore freely. Each sensory play is created to be engaging and supportive, allowing young kids to play with different textures, colors, and materials in a way that feels natural and enjoyable. Tasteopia encourages curiosity and supports meaningful early development through safe sensory plays.

Jessica
Vision in MS

Claire is interested in improving the lives of individuals affected by Multiple Sclerosis (MS) through her research on vision problems associated with the condition. Her work delves into the intricate ways MS impacts visual function, aiming to uncover patterns and potential solutions to address these challenges. By combining scientific inquiry with a patient-centered approach, Claire seeks to contribute to better understanding and management of vision impairments in MS patients. She envisions her findings paving the way for innovative therapies and practical strategies to enhance the daily experiences of those living with this complex neurological condition.

Claire Handoko
Korokita

Driven by the concern for commodity imports and sustainable agriculture, Jio and Sebastian work hand-in-hand to promote an underutilized local crop, koro beans, as a substitution for soy in tempeh and a promising way to increase the welfare of Indonesian farmers. Together, they conducted trials to develop good quality koro-based tempeh and build training materials for farmers to encourage koro plantation. In the future, they plan to bring this to a plantation area in Sumatra, taking one step forward to strengthening the nation’s food security.

Sebastian Ali

Resilience in a Shell

Every year, over 11 million metric tons of plastic enter the ocean and harming marine life and ecosystems. Fionna cares deeply about the environment and loves using art to share important messages. Her artwork, Resilience in a Shell, is a mixed media artwork that shows a person curled up inside a shell, surrounded by real trash like plastic and cans. It represents how nature is trying to protect itself from pollution, but still stays resilient. By using real trash and fabrics, she hopes highlight the growing issue that plastic and other debris harm ocean animals, damage coral reefs, and can release toxic chemicals into the water.
