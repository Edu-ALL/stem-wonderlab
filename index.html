WHAT IS A MAKERSPACE?

A Movement of Student-led Innovation

STEM+ Wonderlab is more than an exhibition — it's a national platform to celebrate youth creativity across Science, Technology, Engineering, Mathematics, and the Arts. Through this event, we spotlight the passion projects of EduALL mentees who have developed impactful work beyond the classroom.

These projects not only strengthen their university applications to top global institutions, but also serve a greater purpose: some of them will be donated to support educationsal needs in remote regions of Indonesia in partnership with Indonesia Mengajar.

We believe in learning that matters — learning that inspires, connects, and gives back.

Indonesia Mengajar

Inspiring the Future Through Teaching

Indonesia Mengajar is a nonprofit initiative that deploys passionate young teachers to serve in under-resourced and remote areas across Indonesia. They aim to close the educational gap and nurture the next generation of Indonesian leaders through quality teaching and community empowerment.

In collaboration with STEM+ Wonderlab, several student projects will be selected and donated to aid the learning experience in these underserved communities — a tangible way students can contribute directly to national impact.

Learn more: www.indonesiamengajar.org
